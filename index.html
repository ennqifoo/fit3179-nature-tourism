What it means

  • The recovery is uneven. Only Queensland is back above its 2019 level (101); the Northern Territory sits at 84. Across regions, 29 of 72 are above 2019, and the desert and outback regions are furthest behind (Kakadu 59, Alice Springs 67).
  • Nature tourism is concentrated. NSW, Victoria and Queensland take four in five regional trips, while Tasmania's North West (+19.5%) and Victoria's Lakes (+17.3%) lead the rebound.
  • Remote places earn more per visitor. The NT's spend per trip ($1,811, up 24%) is the highest of any state, but its visitors swing between the dry-season peak (130) and the wet-season low (67).
  • Why people come. Holidays are 46% of trips, and accommodation, flights and eating out make up 58% of spending. Among international visitors, rented homes (46% of nights) now overtake friends' homes (29%).
